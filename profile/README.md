**RK is a local-first Research Knowledge Platform built around AI.**

RK Workspace brings your AI models, documents, notes and the web into one environment. Work with any supported model, research the web, build and organize knowledge, extend the platform with skills and plugins, and produce reports in your own templates.

**RK Free is your AI knowledge workspace.**

**RK Pro turns it into an epistemic research system** — organizing research into cases where claims are connected to evidence, sources and provenance, with durable sync, backup, collaboration and specialized OSINT capabilities.

**RK helps you work with knowledge. RK Pro helps you establish what you know — and why.**
